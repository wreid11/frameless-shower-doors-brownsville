Built for Rio Grande Valley Bathrooms

Brownsville humidity and hard water are rough on framed shower doors — the metal channels trap scum, mildew, and mineral gunk. Frameless glass has no channels to gunk up: squeegee it after a shower and it stays showroom-clear. That's why frameless is the upgrade local remodelers recommend first.

  • No frames = no mildew traps, easier cleaning in coastal humidity
  • Clear glass opens up smaller Valley bathrooms instantly
  • 3/8" and 1/2" tempered safety glass — strong and code-required
  • Hydrophobic coating options that fight hard-water spotting
  • Hardware in matte black, brushed nickel, chrome, or gold
