Glass, Hardware, and Options

Every enclosure is made to measure. The decisions that shape your quote:

  • Glass thickness — 3/8" is the standard for most doors and panels; 1/2" heavy glass is the luxury option with a noticeably more substantial feel and fewer hardware touches.
  • Clear vs. low-iron — standard tempered glass has a faint green edge; low-iron (starphire-type) glass is ultra-clear and shows your tile true to color. Worth it with white or bold tile.
  • Hardware finishes — matte black, brushed nickel, polished chrome, and gold are the popular picks in Brownsville remodels right now. Match your faucets or deliberately contrast them.
  • Protective coating — a hydrophobic treatment helps water bead off — a genuine upgrade in the Rio Grande Valley's hard-water country.
  • Notches and cutouts — bench cutouts, clamp notches, headerless designs: custom fabrication handles what stock doors can't.

Frameless Shower Doors FAQ — Brownsville Edition

How much do frameless shower doors cost in Brownsville, TX?

Most custom frameless shower projects in the Brownsville area land in the four figures — commonly roughly $900 to $2,500 installed, depending on the glass size and thickness, hardware finish, and how many panels your layout needs. A single fixed panel costs less than a full inline enclosure. Your free quote gives you an exact number before anyone orders glass.

Is frameless glass safe?

Yes. Frameless shower doors are made from tempered safety glass, which is several times stronger than ordinary glass and, if it ever breaks, crumbles into small dull granules instead of sharp shards. This is the same safety standard used in car side windows, and it is required around showers and tubs.

What is the difference between frameless and semi-frameless shower doors?

Frameless doors use thick 3/8-inch or 1/2-inch glass that stands on minimal hardware with no metal framing around the panels. Semi-frameless doors use thinner glass with some framing, usually around the top or edges, and cost less. Frameless looks more open and cleans easier; semi-frameless is the budget-friendly middle ground.

How long does installation take?

The in-home measure takes about 30 minutes. Fabrication of custom glass typically takes one to two weeks. The install itself is usually done in a few hours in a single visit, and you can use the shower about 24 hours later once the sealant cures.

How do I keep hard water spots off frameless glass in the Rio Grande Valley?

Brownsville's water is mineral-heavy, so squeegee the glass after each shower and keep a microfiber cloth handy. Ask your installer about a hydrophobic protective coating — it makes water bead and roll off and stretches the time between cleanings from days to weeks. A 50/50 vinegar-and-water spray cuts the spots that do appear.

Can I put frameless doors on my existing tub or shower?

Often, yes. Tub-to-shower conversions and replacements of old framed doors are routine projects. The key is the condition of the walls and curb: tile and surfaces need to be plumb and solid for heavy glass to mount safely. An installer confirms this during the free in-home measure.
