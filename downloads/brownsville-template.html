Quick Quality Checklist

Ask every contractor about these:

  • Glass thickness: Is it 6mm or 8mm? (8mm is more durable)
  • Hardware: Stainless steel or chrome? Soft-close hinges?
  • Seals: Do they use quality silicone or caulk?
  • Installation: Who handles prep and waterproofing?
  • Timeline: When can they start? How long does it take?
