Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026. This policy covers showerdoorsbrownsville.com and every quote request submitted through it. Plain English, no legalese.

What we collect

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, city or ZIP, phone number (optional), email address (optional), the project type you need, and any shower details, dimensions, or photo link you choose to add. Phone and email are optional individually, but we need at least one so an installer can reach you. We collect nothing else — no tracking pixels, no analytics scripts, no cookies, no browser fingerprinting.

Why we collect it

One reason: to route your free-quote request to a single vetted local glass installer so they can contact you with a price. That is the entire service. Your details go into our internal leads log and to the one installer handling your request.

Who sees it

The site owner and, for your request only, the one local installer we forward it to. Nobody else. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with third parties — not marketers, not data brokers, not "a dozen companies." One request, one installer, that's it.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We never add you to mailing lists. We never call you for anything except the quote you asked for. We never keep your data around after it has served its purpose if you ask us to delete it.

Data retention and your choices

Quote requests are kept only as long as needed to route your job and follow up on it. You can ask us at any time to show you what we stored about you or delete it — email us and we'll handle it promptly. Submitting the form is voluntary; you are never required to book anything.

Questions or requests

Use the quote form and mention "privacy" in the project type notes, or write to us — contact details are on the about page. We'll respond within one business day.
